Door Repair in Cambridge

Cambridge has an unusually wide range of door types — Colonial and Victorian residential entries, early-20th-century institutional doors on university buildings, and triple-decker wood entries throughout Cambridgeport, East Cambridge, and Mid-Cambridge. What they have in common: most are worth repairing rather than replacing, and most have been through decades of deferred maintenance that compound a simple problem into several.

Our work at MIT included a full restoration of a landmark campus entry: stripped, patched, re-hung, and finished to institutional standard. The building's facilities team needed a repair that would hold up to heavy daily use — we re-mortised the hinges, rebuilt the threshold, and applied a durable spar varnish topcoat rated for high-traffic exterior applications.

For residential Cambridge — a sticking entry on a rental property, a peeling front door on a condo, a seized deadbolt on a Victorian — the repair approach is the same: diagnose the root cause first, fix it properly, and leave the owner with a door that functions and looks right.
