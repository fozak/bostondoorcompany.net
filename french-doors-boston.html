French Door Repair in Boston

French doors are among the most demanding doors to maintain in New England — the combination of large glass panels, slender wood stiles, and two active leaves means they're exposed to weather on every face and prone to racking, swelling, and finish failure faster than a standard entry door.

At 90 Park Street in Brookline, we serviced a landmark vestibule entry: a dark-painted double door set within a stone arch surround, with a large arched transom above and an interior vestibule door behind it. The work covered finish maintenance on the entryway, full hinge replacement (six hinges), a new door closer, and a topcoated threshold — bringing both leaves back to smooth, weathertight operation.

Whether your French doors are interior between rooms, exterior patio doors, or a formal entry set like this one, the repair approach is the same: diagnose the root cause first — racking frame, swollen wood, failed weatherstrip, seized hardware — and fix it properly rather than patching over it.

Common French Door Problems We Fix
  • Doors sticking, binding, or dragging on the floor
  • Failed finish — peeling, checking, graying wood
  • Hinge replacement and re-mortising
  • Door closer installation and adjustment
  • Threshold repair and refinishing
  • Weatherstrip replacement — head, jambs, and astragal
  • Glass panel glazing and putty repair
  • Full strip and refinish — oil varnish or paint
