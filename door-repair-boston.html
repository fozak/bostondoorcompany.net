Door Repair Done Right

Most door problems in Boston come down to the same handful of causes — wood movement from New England winters, deferred maintenance on finish and weatherstripping, hardware that's never been serviced, or structural damage from water sitting at the sill. We diagnose the root cause and fix it properly, so the repair lasts.

We work on historic hardwood doors, painted wood doors, French door sets, and institutional entries — anything worth saving rather than replacing. Our projects include a landmark Victorian estate in Jamaica Plain, a Back Bay brownstone on Marlborough Street, and institutional entries at MIT.
