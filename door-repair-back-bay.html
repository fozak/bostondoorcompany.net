Back Bay Door Repair Done Right

Back Bay brownstones have some of the finest original entry doors in Boston — hardwood doors with ornate panel profiles, period hardware, and transoms that define the streetscape. They also have some of the most demanding maintenance requirements: south- and west-facing facades absorb full sun and full weather, finish fails faster, and wood movement from Boston winters is relentless.

At 71 Marlborough Street, we performed a full restoration of the historic entry door: stripped to bare wood, all panels repaired, original hardware cleaned and restored, re-hung in the frame, and finished with Epifanes oil varnish. The result is a door that looks and operates the way it was built to — and that will hold up through the next decade of Boston winters with proper maintenance.

We work on every block of Back Bay — Marlborough, Commonwealth, Beacon, Newbury, Boylston — and we understand the architectural standards these buildings require. Most condo associations and historic district regulations in Back Bay call for repair rather than replacement, which is exactly what we do.
